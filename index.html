01 About

A demo is easy. I care about what still works at 2 a.m. on a highway.

I'm a Software Engineer (AI/ML) at Arya Omnitalk Wireless Solutions, part of the Arvind Group. I build computer vision systems for India's highways that run around the clock on real cameras.

Most of my work is about the messy parts: PTZ cameras that move, headlights at night, riders who look like pedestrians, small objects far from the camera, cameras that drop off the network. My VIDS deployment sees 50,000+ unique vehicles a day across a ~100 km corridor at 90%+ ANPR accuracy. I've also scaled it from a small Jetson at one gantry to 12 cameras on one RTX 5060 Ti, and TMCS runs 26 camera streams at once.

Before this I led my team to AIR 1 at Smart India Hackathon 2023, interned at IBM and MathWorks, and built an alumni portal my college still uses. B.E. in Electronics & Telecommunication, SPPU, 2025. On the side I run MHADA Pune Explorer, a housing-lottery guide that got 141K Google clicks in its first three months.

03 Flagship work · Arya Omnitalk

VIDS — edge AI that logs every vehicle on the highway, not just the violators.

VIDS (Video Incident Detection System) is the highway enforcement and incident system I build at Arya Omnitalk. A GPU box at each gantry reads the cameras, detects and tracks every vehicle, reads its plate, measures its speed and checks it for violations. The control room gets checked events with evidence, not raw video.

It records every vehicle that passes, not only violators, so the road operator gets full traffic counts and a way to audit toll records. The records are used as legal evidence, so being correct comes first.

Also Arya Omnitalk

TMCS — turning existing highway cameras into automatic incident detectors

TMCS watches live PTZ camera feeds along NHAI ATMS corridors and raises incidents on its own: stopped or parked vehicles, wrong-way driving, people, animals and debris on the road, and queues. It handles moving PTZ cameras, glare at night and riders who look like pedestrians, and every event comes with write-once, SHA-256-hashed evidence.
