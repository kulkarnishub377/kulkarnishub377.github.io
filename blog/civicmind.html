1. The Challenge

We needed a system that acts as a "smart city brain," actively detecting crises, predicting downstream risks, and providing fully explainable recommendations. It had to transcend simple dashboards and move into Decision Intelligence.

2. Multi-Agent AI Orchestration

Instead of a single monolithic model, CivicMind employs a Multi-Agent architecture using Google Gemini 2.5 and Vertex AI. Four distinct agents collaborate in real-time:

  • EcoWatch: Monitors environmental anomalies (air quality, flood risks).
  • TransitFlow: Analyzes traffic congestion and public transit bottlenecks.
  • CivicVoice: Processes citizen feedback and sentiment from 311 calls.
  • Strategy Agent: Correlates findings from the other three agents to generate holistic policy recommendations.

3. Digital Twin Simulator

We developed a "What-If" simulator allowing leaders to test emergency responses and visualize risk reduction before deploying real resources. Built with Next.js 14 and Chart.js, the frontend provides a glassmorphic, responsive interface to manipulate variables and observe predicted outcomes.

4. Explainable AI (XAI)

In public policy, a black-box model is a liability. We integrated driver analysis and confidence bounds into all AI outputs to ensure complete transparency and civic trust. Every recommendation provides a trace of which data points (and which agent) contributed to the conclusion.
