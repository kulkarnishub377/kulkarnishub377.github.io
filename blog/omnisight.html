1. Executive Summary

OmniSight is designed to ingest and correlate high-velocity signals from diverse edge sources—including RTSP Cameras, IoT Sensors, Microphones, and standard operating procedure (SOP) Documents. It uses a high-end FastAPI backend and Docker-based deployment.

2. Core Use Cases

Smart Manufacturing: Correlates IoT temperature spikes with audio grinding anomalies, querying the machine's maintenance manual using RAG, and determining a high risk of catastrophic failure.

Secure Facility Monitoring: A badge-swipe is registered at a secure door, but the camera detects two faces (tailgating). OmniSight flags the anomaly, cross-references the access control policy, and alerts the guard station.

3. Agentic Workflow Engine

OmniSight moves beyond simple rules-based alerts by utilizing a Deterministic Agentic Runtime composed of specialized AI personas:

  • Observer: Continuously monitors the event stream.
  • Investigator: Groups related anomalies within specific time-windows.
  • Retriever: Queries the Qdrant/Postgres vector database for relevant SOPs.
  • Planner: Formulates a step-by-step resolution plan.
  • Safety Guard: A deterministic policy engine reviewing suggestions.
  • Action Executor: Dispatches approved actions (webhooks, Jira tickets).
